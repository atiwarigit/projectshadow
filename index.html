Shadow drops

How help moves

The desk periodically runs a shadow drop — a quiet round of aid pulled from the community pool. Anonymous or credited is the receiver's call, never a condition of getting it. Some drops go to a person; some go to whoever lifted a person.

  • GAS MONEYA small transfer to cover the thing that can't wait.
  • PRINT TOOLSAccess, credits, or software so someone can do the work.
  • ZEC SUPPORTA small amount of ZEC, sent shielded or transparent.
  • RESOURCE LINKSThe right doc, the right person, the right door.
  • PUBLIC CREDITA named thank-you for someone who helped someone else.
